        The bar under each train shows how it's running now: green on time, amber late, red 10+ minutes late or cancelled. Before there's news of it, the bar shows how punctual it has been lately (green 90%+ on time, amber 75%+, red less), and grey means no record yet. The percentage is how often it has left within 5 minutes on recent days like today, from TrainTimes' own records. Live running from Network Rail's train movement feed. Times are UK time.
