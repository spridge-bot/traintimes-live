      Use my location

      Near work, the Next page shows trains home, and near home, trains to work, whatever the time. The map's departures open on the station you're at. Your location stays on your phone, except your train's position while you're on one, if you share it (below).

      Sharing

      Share my train's position

      On a train, TrainTimes knows where it really is from your phone. Share that, so people waiting for it see it too (and you see theirs before you board). Only the train and where it is are sent, never who you are, and they're forgotten after two minutes.
