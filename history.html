Delay Repay is paid on how late you arrive against the timetable: 15-29 minutes 25% of a single, 30-59 minutes 50%, 60-119 minutes 100%, 2 hours or more the full return. For a cancelled train, claim for how late you actually got there. Claims are made with the operator of the delayed train, within 28 days, with a photo of your ticket. TrainTimes can't send the claim for you, but everything the form asks for is here to copy.
